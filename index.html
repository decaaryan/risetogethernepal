About Us

Standing together for a stronger, fairer Nepal.

At Rise Together Nepal, we believe that real, lasting change doesn’t come from the top down—it starts right here in our own neighborhoods, led by the people who live in them. Our mission is to give a loud, clear platform to the voices that are so often left unheard, showing up for the individuals and families who need it most. Because we know that building a stronger community means looking at the whole picture, our hands-on work spans across education, health, social justice, and immediate disaster relief. We aren't looking for quick, temporary fixes; whether we are supporting a single family through a tough time or launching broader neighborhood projects, our focus is always on creating sustainable change that lets communities thrive on their own terms. When you look at our logo, you see rising peaks—but to us, those peaks represent exactly what happens when a community lifts each other up.
